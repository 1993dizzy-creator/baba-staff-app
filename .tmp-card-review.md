## Behavior

CARD_FEE_PENDING is a warning. Negative clearing, allocation mismatch and legacy unallocated deposits remain blockers.
Closed prior sale months are processed oldest first. Above 2.5%: continue principal FIFO. Within 1.8%-2.5%: confirm actual remaining fee. Overshoot: preserve rounded 2.15% and allocate remaining deposit to later sale months. Below 1.8% or insufficient next-month capacity: no writes.
Fee expense recognition_month remains the sale month; confirmed_at and clearing movement business date use actual finalization time/day. Only card_clearing is reduced; fee creates no bank movement. Deposit allocations sum exactly to principal. Existing confirmed fees are not duplicated; cancelled history remains intact.
Snapshots retain historical holdings; expected post-close fee expense is normalized only for snapshot drift comparison, while current historical P&L reflects the final fee.
Manual confirmation UI removed; compatibility RPC and cancellation/history preserved.
Regular khac/PartnerSelect, historical payable read-only, PaymentVerification and inventory category policy retained.
